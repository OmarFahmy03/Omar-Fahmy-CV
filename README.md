## ✨ Features

- 🖥 **Responsive Design** - Viewable across all devices (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop)
- 🎨 **Blue Dark Theme** - Sleek, dark, and modern design
- 🛠 **Project Showcase** - Displaying projects with descriptions, technologies used, and links to live demos
- ✍️ **Experience & Education** - A timeline showcasing my professional experience and education background
- 📈 **Skills Overview** - Highlighting my key technical skills and areas of expertise
- 🎯 **Interactive CV** - Dynamic, filterable CV experience section
- 📤 **Contact Form** - A simple contact form to get in touch with me
- 🎥 **Github & Linkedin Links** - Direct links to my Tech content on Github & Linkedin
- 🧩 **Smooth Animations** - Implemented with Framer Motion for a dynamic and engaging experience
- 🖼 **Media Gallery** - Showcasing some of my personal projects and professional achievements
- **TypeScript support added** for enhanced experience and type safety

## ✅ Features

- 🎨 **Modern Design**: Inspired by the "Night Blue" color palette, providing a sleek and professional look with dark mode support.
- 💻 **Dynamic CV Section**: A dedicated section displaying your professional education, experience, and skills dynamically, using animations and transitions.
- 🖋️ **Downloadable Resume**: Adding functionality to download a PDF version of my resume directly from the portfolio.
- 📄 **Projects Section**: A Netflix-style slider for showcasing your projects, with sorting functionality.
- 📞 **Contact Form**: A fully functional contact form with validation and submission features, allowing users to reach out directly.
- 📚 **Education and Experience**: Dynamically displayed educational background and work experience through an interactive component.
- 🖥️ **Responsive Design**: Optimized for both desktop and mobile, ensuring the portfolio looks great on all devices.
- 🌑 **Dark Mode**: Implemented dark mode using a custom color scheme (`Night Blue`), providing a comfortable viewing experience in low-light environments.
- 🧑‍💼 **Skills Section**: Detailed skills with icons and progress bars to showcase your technical abilities.
- 📈 **Animations and Transitions**: Smooth, subtle animations for better user interaction, particularly for the CV, projects, and education sections.
- 🔗 **External Links**: Easily accessible links to your GitHub, LinkedIn, and other professional platforms.
- 📊 **Filterable Project Display**: Projects can be filtered based on type, making it easy to navigate.
- 🧩 **Reusable Components**: Components such as cards, buttons, forms, and sliders are designed for reusability to maintain consistency and ease of scalability.
- 🧑‍💻 **Code Reusability**: Used modular approaches like custom hooks, reusable components, and dynamic arrays to ensure clean, maintainable, and scalable code.
